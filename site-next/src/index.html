░▒▓█ 01 Features

Everything a BBS needs

Laid out the way a board lists its files. The keys are BadTerm's own: Alt with a letter, or Cmd on a Mac, and each can be moved in Settings.

File area 1: BadTerm9 files
FilenameKeyDescription
  • PIXELS.ANSAlt-W

    Pixel-exact ANSI. BadTerm draws its own screen in the IBM VGA fonts, 8 or 9 dots wide, from 80x25 to 132x52. iCE colors, blinking, 256 and 24-bit color, sixel pictures, and SyncTERM's palettes and the fonts BBSes send.

  • PHOSPHOR.CRTAlt-P

    Phosphor mode. The look of a CRT: glowing text, scanlines and curved glass, in color or on a green or amber monitor, for every BBS or just the ones you choose.

  • CONNECT.ANYAlt-L

    Connect any way. Telnet, telnet over TLS, raw TCP and RLogin, and SSH with keys, your agent or a password. Passwords can live in your system's keychain.

  • ATDT.MDMAlt-R

    Dial a real modem. Point BadTerm at a modem on a serial port, a WiFi modem or tcpser, and it dials the number: ATDT, CONNECT, and a redial when the line is BUSY.

  • ZMODEM.XFRAlt-U

    File transfers. ZMODEM starts by itself and resumes where it stopped. YMODEM, YMODEM-G, XMODEM and Kermit are a keypress away. Drop files on the window to upload.

  • DIALER.DIRAlt-D

    A real dialing directory. Search, sort, pin favorites, auto-login, macros and settings for each BBS. Bring your list from SyncTERM, NetRunner, Qodem, EtherTerm, MagiTerm or the Telnet BBS Guide.

  • SCROLL.BAKAlt-B

    Keep everything. 10,000 lines of scrollback, even of screens the BBS cleared. Capture every call as ANSI or as a text log, save the screen as .ans or .png, and view art with its SAUCE credits.

  • DOORS.SNDAlt-M

    Doors with pictures and sound. Synchronet's graphical doors draw their pictures, play their sound and music, and take the mouse, just as in SyncTERM. ANSI music plays as a PC speaker would.

  • COLORS.THMAlt-O

    Your colors. Thirteen color schemes for BadTerm's own screens, Bad Cluster's and Dark Dracula among them, or color every part yourself, as in QModem and Telix. Share yours as a theme file.

░▒▓█ 02 Systems

Every kind of board

Not just ANSI. BadTerm speaks the graphics and character sets of the other machines the BBS scene ran on, and the pictures, sound and fonts of today's boards.

Also BBC Micro Mode 7, AVATAR (AVT/0+) for Opus and Maximus boards, the Amiga's Topaz and MicroKnight fonts, and UTF-8.
